📝 Install Cursor Editor

×

🌐 Step 1: Go to Cursor Website

Visit: https://cursor.so

💻 Step 2: Download for Your System

For Windows:

Click "Download for Windows"

For Mac:

Click "Download for macOS"

⚙️ Step 3: Install Cursor

  1. Open the downloaded file
  2. Follow the install steps (click "Next" a few times!)

🔐 Step 4: Sign In with Google

  1. After opening Cursor, look for the sign-in option
  2. Click on "Sign in with Google"
  3. Select your Google account
  4. Grant necessary permissions
  5. You're all set! 🎉

×

🌐 Step 1: Go to ChatGPT Website

Visit: https://chat.openai.com

📝 Step 2: Create an Account

  1. Click "Sign up" button
  2. Enter your email address
  3. Create a password
  4. Verify your email address

⚙️ Step 3: Complete Setup

  1. Enter your name and birth date
  2. Choose your use case
  3. Start chatting with ChatGPT!
